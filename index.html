Punch Pop

Fit a shape on the blocks, punch it through, and watch the colours fall into chains. A free puzzle game for iPhone.

Coming soon to the App Store

How to play

  • Drag a shape onto the board. Every square has to sit on a block.
  • Everything under the shape pops, and the blocks above fall.
  • Four or more touching blocks of one colour clear, and the next fall can clear again.
  • Every punch fills the meter under the board. When it's full, a new row pushes in. Don't let the stack reach the top.

Common questions

Is my progress saved? Yes. Your best score, settings and any run in progress are saved on your iPhone. If you leave mid-game, tap Continue on the home screen.

Will I lose my best score if I delete the app? Yes. Everything is stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes it too.

Can I play offline? Yes, the whole game works without a connection.

Colours are hard to tell apart. Turn on Settings → Colour Symbols to add a symbol to every block.
