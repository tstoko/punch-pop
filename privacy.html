Punch Pop privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026. Applies to Punch Pop for iPhone.

In short: Punch Pop has no accounts and we don't collect your personal data ourselves. The game shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional ad between runs, and an optional ad you can choose to watch at game over for a second chance. Google collects some device and usage data to show ads and prevent fraud. A one-off Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads between runs. We don't ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Who we are

Punch Pop is made by Tom Stokolenko, publishing as stoko ("we"). You can contact us at tstokolenko@outlook.com.

What the game stores on your device

Your best score, settings, the run you're playing and your Learn to Play progress are saved on your iPhone only. We can't see them, and deleting the app deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of ad. Sometimes, when you start another run, an ad plays first; these never appear during a run, in your first session or in your first few runs. When a run ends, the game may also offer a WATCH AD button for a second chance, which plays an ad only if you tap it. To load and show ads, the game includes Google's Mobile Ads software, which may collect:

  • device and app identifiers, limited to this app or developer (we don't ask for the advertising identifier used for cross-app tracking)
  • approximate location, worked out from your IP address
  • information about the ads you see and tap
  • app performance and crash information

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, keep ads safe and prevent fraud. Google is responsible for that data under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

We receive only combined reports from Google (for example, how many ads were shown). They don't identify you.

Your choices

  • Remove Ads. A one-off purchase in the game turns off the ads between runs. The WATCH AD second chance stays available, and still plays only if you tap it.
  • Consent in the UK, EEA and Switzerland. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form appears the first time you open the game. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • Tracking. Punch Pop never asks for permission to track you, so your iPhone's advertising identifier isn't shared with us or Google.

Purchases

Remove Ads is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or Apple ID details. The game asks the App Store only whether this Apple ID owns Remove Ads, and keeps the answer on your iPhone. Restore in Settings brings it back on a new iPhone.

Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous, combined statistics such as download counts and crash reports. See Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Punch Pop is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children. Ads are requested with Google's family-friendly content rating.

Your rights

We don't hold personal data about you, so there's nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. For data collected by Google, use the links above or Google's My Ad Center. You can always email us with questions.

Changes

If we change what the game collects, we'll update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: tstokolenko@outlook.com.
